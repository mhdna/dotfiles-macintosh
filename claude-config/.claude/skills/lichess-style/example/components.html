Returns policy

Medicine can come back within 7 days if the box is sealed and the receipt is shown.

What can't come back

  • Opened boxes and blister strips
  • Fridge items: insulin, vaccines, eye drops once opened
  • Anything bought more than 7 days ago

Refunds

Cash sales are refunded in cash. Card sales go back to the card within 5 working days. Insurance sales are corrected with the insurer, and the customer pays nothing extra.

The pharmacist on duty can approve a return up to $50. Above that, the owner approves it.

Recording a return

Open the sale, press Return, tick the items coming back and pick a reason. Stock goes back up only for sealed items; damaged ones are recorded as a loss.
